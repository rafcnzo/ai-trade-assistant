# Indeks referensi

Peta bab buku yang sudah dikonversi (`referensi/buku/<slug>/bab-NN.md`, pribadi, tidak di-commit). Agent membaca file ini lewat
`mcp_paper_baca_memori("indeks")`, mencari isi lewat `mcp_paper_cari_referensi(kata kunci)`, dan membaca bab lewat
`mcp_paper_baca_referensi(slug, bab)`. Kolom "Bab" = nomor file `bab-NN`; "Halaman" = halaman PDF (untuk buku pertama,
halaman cetak = halaman PDF − 36). Diperbarui 2026-10-11 oleh Claude Code setelah konversi.

## goodcrypto-pola-chart — GoodCrypto, *Chart Patterns for Crypto Trading* (presentasi)

| Bab | Judul | Halaman | Ringkasan 1 kalimat | Kata kunci |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Chart Patterns for Crypto Trading (cheat sheet) | 1–2 | Pengantar pola reversal vs continuation + cheat sheet bullish/bearish | chart patterns, reversal, continuation, cheat sheet, bullish, bearish, volume |
| 02 | Reversal Patterns | 3–9 | Diamond, double top/bottom, cup & handle, rounded bottom, rising/falling wedge: aturan breakout dan target | diamond, double top, double bottom, cup and handle, rounded bottom, rising wedge, falling wedge, breakout, price target, volume confirmation |
| 03 | Continuation Patterns | 10–13 | Ascending/descending/symmetrical triangle dan rectangle; target = lebar bukaan pola dari titik breakout; rectangle momentum lebih kecil | continuation, ascending triangle, descending triangle, symmetrical triangle, rectangle, breakout, momentum, price target |

Daftar pola (bab 02–03): Diamond top/bottom (reversal), Double top (bearish reversal), Double/triple bottom (bullish reversal),
Cup & handle (bullish reversal), Rounded bottom (bullish reversal), Rising wedge (reversal bearish dari tren naik; continuation
bearish dalam tren turun), Falling wedge (reversal bullish dari tren turun; continuation bullish dalam tren naik), Ascending
triangle (continuation bullish), Descending triangle (continuation bearish), Symmetrical triangle (continuation searah tren asal),
Rectangle (continuation, momentum kecil). Catatan: sumbernya sendiri tidak konsisten di hal. 1 dan 8 soal rising wedge; hal. 13
berisi promo aplikasi, bukan materi.
